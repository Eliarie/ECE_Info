ECE Papper Web is a platform for discovering, annotating, and discussing electrical and computer engineering research papers.
- Aggregate papers from arXiv, IEEE Xplore, and ACM Digital Library with automated metadata extraction.
- Offer semantic full-text search and personalized recommendation feeds based on user interests.
- Enable inline annotations, public/private comments, and threaded discussions on papers.
- Visualize citation graphs, co-authorship networks, and emerging research trends.
- Allow one-click export of bibliographies, reading lists, and paper summaries.
